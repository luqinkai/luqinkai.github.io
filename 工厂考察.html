工厂概况 | Factory Overview

我们的合作工厂拥有先进的生产设备和严格的质量管理体系，确保产品质量符合国际标准。工厂占地面积达5000平方米，拥有多条生产线，年生产能力达到1000万件。

The factory strictly implements the ISO13485 quality management system. From raw material procurement to finished product delivery, each link has professional personnel for quality inspection to ensure stable and reliable product quality.
